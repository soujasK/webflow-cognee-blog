Turning Spoken Words into Knowledge Graphs: The tl;dv Connector

By Soujas K • October 2026 • Mergetober 2026 tl;dv REST API Cognee Community

Remote engineering organizations spend hundreds of hours every month in Zoom and Google Meet calls discussing roadmap decisions, customer discovery feedback, and sprint retrospectives.

Once a meeting concludes, that context is effectively lost or locked inside hour-long video recordings. The cognee-community-connector-tldv extracts meeting recordings, speaker-attributed transcripts, and AI-generated summary notes, transforming ephemeral spoken dialogue into searchable semantic graph nodes.

Data Pipeline: tl;dv Meetings to Cognee Graph
tl;dv Public REST API Meetings, Transcripts & Notes
➔
Transcript Formatter Speaker Turns & Takeaways
➔
Cognee Cognify Decision & Stakeholder Graph
➔
Agent Reasoning Action Item & Decision Recall

Technical Architecture

  • Multi-Part Document Synthesis: Combines meeting metadata (organizer, date, duration), executive summaries, action item checklists, and timestamped speaker dialogue into structured Markdown.
  • Incremental Time-Window Sync: The connector accepts a since_date ISO timestamp, querying only recordings created or modified since the last pipeline run.
  • Speaker Attribution in Entity Graph: Transcripts tag individual speakers (e.g. [04:12] Alice:), allowing Cognee's entity extraction to correlate decisions with specific stakeholders.

Production Usage

import cognee
from cognee_community_connector_tldv import tldv_source

# Ingest recent sprint planning and customer discovery calls
source = tldv_source(
    api_key="TLDV_API_KEY",
    since_date="2026-10-01T00:00:00Z",
    include_transcripts=True,
    include_notes=True
)

await cognee.add(source)
await cognee.cognify()

# Ask your AI agent what happened in meetings
summary = await cognee.search("What action items were assigned to Bob during Monday's sprint planning?")
print(summary)
