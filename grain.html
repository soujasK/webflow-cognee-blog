Video Highlights & Meeting Decisions: Grain Connector for Cognee

Transforming recorded video meetings, AI highlights, sub-second video clip boundaries, and speaker transcripts into structured graph memory.
Issue #4739 11/11 Tests Passing Grain REST API DOCUMENT_SOURCE_ATTR = "grain" Author: Soujas K

1. Capturing Conversational Intelligence

Grain enables customer success, product, and sales teams to record calls, create AI highlights, and generate timestamped clip summaries. While transcripts contain raw dialogue, highlights reflect human and AI curated consensus.

Ingesting Grain into Cognee extracts action items, customer objections, and technical requirements into knowledge graph nodes, linking decisions directly back to the exact video timestamp.

2. Architectural Edge Cases Handled

  • Sub-Second Highlight Precision: Grain offsets operate on fractional millisecond boundaries. Converted to human-readable HH:MM:SS format while preserving exact offsets in document metadata.
  • In-Flight Transcript State: Freshly uploaded recordings may have highlights generated before transcripts complete speech-to-text processing. The connector safely ingests available metadata without raising KeyError.
  • Unidentified Speaker Normalization: Guest attendees without account profiles safely default to Unknown Speaker to prevent markdown parsing errors.
  • Speaker Deduplication Stability: Multi-turn conversations are deduplicated in participant lists.
  • Deleted Clip Omission: Clips with empty text or deleted flags are excluded to prevent polluting LLM contexts.
